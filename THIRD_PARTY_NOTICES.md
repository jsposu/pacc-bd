# osu!lazer slider references

The difficulty spectrum and slider colour behaviour in `src/difficultyColours.ts`
are adapted from ppy/osu's `osu.Game/Graphics/OsuColour.cs` and
`osu.Game/Screens/Select/FilterControl.DifficultyRangeSlider.cs`.
The sheared range slider appearance is based on
`osu.Game/Graphics/UserInterface/ShearedRangeSlider.cs`.
Source: https://github.com/ppy/osu (accessed 2026-11-02).
BPM and other statistic gradients are PACC-BD adaptations, not official osu! scales.
